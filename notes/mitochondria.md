# Mitochondria Notes

## Function
Known as the powerhouse of the cell, mitochondria generate most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions through cellular respiration.

## Structure
1. **Outer Membrane**: Freely permeable to small molecules.
2. **Inner Membrane**: Folded into cristae to increase surface area; contains the electron transport chain and ATP synthase.
3. **Matrix**: Space enclosed by the inner membrane; contains mitochondrial DNA, ribosomes, and enzymes for the Krebs cycle.

## Cellular Respiration Steps
1. Glycolysis (occurs in the cytoplasm)
2. Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle) in the mitochondrial matrix
3. Oxidative Phosphorylation (Electron Transport Chain) across the inner mitochondrial membrane
