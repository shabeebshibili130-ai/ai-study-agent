# Chemistry: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding & Organic Chemistry

## 1. Atomic Structure & Periodic Trends
- **Quantum Numbers**:
  - Principal ($n$): Energy level ($1, 2, 3\dots$).
  - Angular momentum ($l$): Orbital shape ($0 \le l \le n-1$; $s, p, d, f$).
  - Magnetic ($m_l$): Spatial orientation ($-l \le m_l \le +l$).
  - Spin ($m_s$): Electron spin ($+\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$).
- **Periodic Trends**:
  - **Electronegativity**: Increases across a period ($\to$), decreases down a group ($\downarrow$). (Fluorine is highest: 3.98).
  - **Ionization Energy**: Energy required to remove an electron. Increases across ($\to$), decreases down ($\downarrow$).
  - **Atomic Radius**: Decreases across ($\to$) due to increasing effective nuclear charge $Z_{\text{eff}}$, increases down ($\downarrow$).

## 2. Chemical Bonding & Molecular Geometry
- **Ionic vs. Covalent vs. Metallic**:
  - Ionic: Complete electron transfer ($\Delta\chi > 1.7$). High melting points, conductive in molten/aqueous states.
  - Covalent: Sharing of electron pairs. Polar vs. Nonpolar based on dipole moments.
- **VSEPR Theory & Hybridization**:
  - $sp$: Linear ($180^\circ$, e.g., $\text{CO}_2, \text{C}_2\text{H}_2$).
  - $sp^2$: Trigonal planar ($120^\circ$, e.g., $\text{BF}_3, \text{C}_2\text{H}_4$).
  - $sp^3$: Tetrahedral ($109.5^\circ$, e.g., $\text{CH}_4$, bent $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ at $104.5^\circ$, trigonal pyramidal $\text{NH}_3$ at $107^\circ$).

## 3. Organic Chemistry & Reaction Mechanisms
- **Functional Groups**:
  - Alcohols ($-\text{OH}$), Aldehydes ($-\text{CHO}$), Ketones ($-\text{CO}-$), Carboxylic Acids ($-\text{COOH}$), Esters ($-\text{COOR}$), Amines ($-\text{NH}_2$).
- **Nucleophilic Substitution**:
  - $\text{S}_\text{N}1$: Two-step mechanism via carbocation intermediate. Rate $= k[\text{substrate}]$. Racemization. Favored by tertiary substrates and polar protic solvents.
  - $\text{S}_\text{N}2$: One-step concerted backside attack. Rate $= k[\text{substrate}][\text{nucleophile}]$. Walden inversion. Favored by primary substrates and polar aprotic solvents.
- **Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS)**: Benzene reacts with electrophiles (nitration, halogenation, Friedel-Crafts alkylation/acylation).

## 4. Thermodynamics & Chemical Equilibrium
- **Gibbs Free Energy**: $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$. Spontaneous when $\Delta G < 0$.
- **Equilibrium Constant ($K_{\text{eq}}$)**: $\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K$.
- **Le Chatelier's Principle**: When a stress is applied to a system at equilibrium, the system adjusts to relieve the stress.
