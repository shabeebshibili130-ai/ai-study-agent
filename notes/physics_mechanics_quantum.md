# Physics: Classical Mechanics & Modern Physics

## 1. Classical Mechanics
- **Newton's Laws of Motion**:
  1. **First Law (Inertia)**: An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a net external force: $\sum \vec{F} = 0 \implies \vec{v} = \text{const}$.
  2. **Second Law (Momentum & Force)**: $\vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = m\vec{a}$ (for constant mass).
  3. **Third Law (Action-Reaction)**: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction: $\vec{F}_{AB} = -\vec{F}_{BA}$.
- **Work-Energy Theorem**: Net work done equals change in kinetic energy: $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta K = \frac{1}{2}mv_f^2 - \frac{1}{2}mv_i^2$.
- **Conservation of Momentum**: In an isolated system with no external forces, total linear momentum is conserved: $\sum \vec{p}_i = \sum \vec{p}_f$.

## 2. Thermodynamics
- **Zeroth Law**: Defines thermal equilibrium and temperature.
- **First Law**: Energy conservation $\Delta U = Q - W$.
- **Second Law**: Total entropy of an isolated system always increases over time: $\Delta S \ge 0$. Efficiency of heat engine $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}$.

## 3. Electromagnetism (Maxwell's Equations)
1. **Gauss's Law for Electricity**: $\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = \frac{Q_{\text{enc}}}{\varepsilon_0}$
2. **Gauss's Law for Magnetism**: $\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{A} = 0$ (no magnetic monopoles).
3. **Faraday's Law of Induction**: $\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{s} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$
4. **Ampère-Maxwell Law**: $\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{s} = \mu_0 I_{\text{enc}} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$

## 4. Quantum Mechanics & Special Relativity
- **Photoelectric Effect**: $E = h\nu = \Phi + K_{\text{max}}$. Proved quantization of light (photons).
- **de Broglie Wavelength**: Wave-particle duality: $\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$.
- **Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle**: $\Delta x \Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$.
- **Mass-Energy Equivalence**: $E^2 = (pc)^2 + (m_0 c^2)^2 \implies E = mc^2$ (at rest).
